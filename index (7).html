Our Story

About Naxshin

We are a Kurdish-rooted fashion platform connecting local designers, print partners, and customers across the region.

Our Mission

Naxshin was born from a desire to celebrate Kurdish identity through wearable art. We partner with local designers, give them a platform to share their creations, and connect them with customers who want authentic, meaningful clothing. Our name — Naxshin — means "decorated" or "adorned" in Kurdish, and that's exactly what we aim to do: adorn everyday life with beauty and culture.
