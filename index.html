About Naxshin

A Kurdish-rooted fashion platform connecting local designers, print partners, and customers.

Our Mission

Naxshin was born from a desire to celebrate Kurdish identity through wearable art. We partner with local designers, give them a platform to share their creations, and connect them with customers who want authentic, meaningful clothing. Our name — Naxshin — means "decorated" or "adorned" in Kurdish.
